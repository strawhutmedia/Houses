Questions

Are these deals real? +

The sources are 100% real — the federal government and counties genuinely auction seized and forfeited property below market value every week. The listings shown on this demo are representative samples that mirror those sources so you can see how the product works. Wiring the live crawler into this same feed is the next build step.

Where does the data come from? +

US Marshals, US Treasury, FDIC owned real-estate listings, GSA Auctions, GovDeals.com, and county tax-deed sales. EquityScout normalizes all of them into one schema and scores the equity.

What does "built-in equity" mean? +

The gap between the government's opening/auction price and the property's estimated market value. A $90K opening price on a home worth $400K is $310K of built-in equity — before any repairs or resale.

How do I actually bid? +

Open any deal to see the auction date and the source it came from. Each government source has its own registration and bidding process; EquityScout points you straight to the right one instead of making you hunt.
